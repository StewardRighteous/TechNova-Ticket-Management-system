# 03: Executive Dashboard with KPI Ribbons & Volume Breakdowns

**What to build:**
An executive-level operational dashboard demonstrating TechNova's service metrics to prospective client leaders:
1. Executive KPI ribbon displaying Total Active Tickets, Overdue Tickets, SLA Breached / At Risk count (highlighted in crimson), and Average Resolution Time.
2. Interactive Volume Breakdown cards showing Tickets by Intake Channel (WhatsApp, Email, Phone) and Tickets by Department.
3. High-attention actionable ticket table highlighting critical and approaching-SLA tickets with direct navigation to their detail views.
